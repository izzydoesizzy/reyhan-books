Cover Check

Probes every book against every cover source, bypassing all caches, and shows exactly where each cover can come from. Books with a red NONE row have no working source and need a hand-picked URL in COVER_OVERRIDES (v2/config.js). Heads up: Open Library rate-limits ISBN lookups to roughly 100 per 5 minutes, so avoid re-running back-to-back.
